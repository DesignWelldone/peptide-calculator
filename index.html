How to Verify Your Spray Volume

  1. Fill with a known volume of plain water.
  2. Prime the pump, then spray 10 actuations into a container.
  3. Measure the collected liquid accurately.
  4. Divide the measured volume by 10 for actual mL per spray.

Typical Spray Volumes

  • Standard metered pump: about 0.10 mL
  • Fine mist: about 0.05–0.07 mL
  • Large actuator: about 0.12–0.14 mL

How It Works

1. Concentration

Divide vial amount in mcg by liquid volume in mL.

2. Volume Needed

Divide the desired dose by concentration to get mL per dose.

3. Syringe Units

For a U-100 syringe, multiply mL by 100 to get units.

Example

  • 5 mg vial + 2 mL liquid = 2.5 mg/mL
  • 250 mcg target dose = 0.25 mg
  • 0.25 ÷ 2.5 = 0.1 mL
  • 0.1 mL = 10 U-100 units
Research Use Only

This calculator performs arithmetic only. It is not medical advice and does not determine whether a compound, dose, route, or preparation is appropriate.
